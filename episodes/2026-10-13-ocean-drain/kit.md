# What If #2026-10-13 — The Ocean Drained Away in 10 Minutes

**Title:** What if the ocean left… and then came back for you?
**Time scale:** minutes (Minute 1 → Minute 10), collapsing to "0.0s" underwater, then looping to Minute 1.
**Format:** POV, one continuous shot, 68 s (2040 frames @ 30 fps), 1080x1920, no voice-over.
**Hook (0–2 s):** you stand at a pier rail at dawn, hands on the railing. Text on frame 0: **THE OCEAN IS LEAVING / Minute 1.**

## Structure
- Hook (0–3 s): calm dawn, boats, hands on rail, hook text.
- Escalation (3–26 s): the sea drains, pilings appear, you climb down the ladder and walk the seabed, fish flop, gulls flee.
- Twist (38–50 s): a dark line on the horizon is the water coming back — as a wall. Pip (kid in yellow cap, red scarf) points you towards shore. Whip-pan and run.
- Climax (50–62 s): impact, white flash, underwater tumble, "0.0s — hold your breath".
- Loop (62–68 s): you surface, the sea is calm, you float back up to the pier rail: same frame as the first one.
