## Hook (0–2 s)

Frame 0 is already readable, with no fade-in: two POV hands stretched toward a small grey pebble on a garden table, morning light.
On screen: **DAY 1** and *"What if you practiced telekinesis every day?"*
The pebble does nothing, which is the joke and the promise at the same time. Trend codes used: a POV/outcome hook that reads within about 1.5 s, text-first for muted viewers, and a hook that sounds like someone mid-thought rather than an ad.

| Part | Time | Beat |

| Hook | 0–5 s | Day 1: hands out, nothing happens. |
