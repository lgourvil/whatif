# What If #2026-10-14 — The Ocean Left Tonight

**Title:** What if the ocean left tonight?
**Time scale:** accelerating — `Second 1` → `Minute 4` → `Hour 2…20` → `Day 2…30` → then collapses into the decimal scale `0.0s … 9.9s` for the surge → back to `Second 1` (loop).
**Format:** POV, one continuous shot, 72 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, no voice-over, on-screen text only.
**Theme:** nature / physical absurdity. **Original recurring character:** *Marlo* (yellow raincoat, hood-cap with a red-tipped antenna, long red scarf, lantern).

## Hook (0–3 s)
Sunset on the end of a wooden pier. Marlo (right third) points at the sea. Text, readable on frame 0: **SECOND 1 / WHAT IF THE OCEAN LEFT TONIGHT?** Water droplets float *upward* off the sea. Within two seconds the waterline visibly starts to retreat down the pilings.

## Structure
| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–8 s | Pier at sunset, the sea starts leaving — the droplets are rising, not falling |
| Escalation 1 | 8–20 s | Walk to the end of the pier, climb down the ladder hand over hand; sea level drops past us |
| Escalation 2 | 20–32 s | Walk the dry seabed: wreck, bleaching coral, flopping fish, sunset→night time-lapse |
| Reveal | 32–44 s | Stop, tilt the head back: three rivers of water flow up into a giant Moon |
| Escalation 3 | 44–58 s | Turn and run back to the pier; days fly by (sun time-lapse), ground shakes, palette goes alarm-red, FOV widens |
| Twist | 58–65 s | The rivers let go. Dolly-zoom + white flash. The ocean comes home and lifts us up the ladder, underwater, through the surface |
| Loop | 65–72 s | Gasp, calm sea, Marlo points at the horizon again — last frame = first frame |

## Continuous movements (one take, transitions are the camera itself)
Last frame of each prompt is reused as the first frame of the next.

### 1. 0:00–0:08 — Hook on the pier
- **POV:** standing at the end of the pier, golden hour, Marlo at the railing pointing at the sea. Camera drifts forward, a quick glance left, then down at the water retreating along the pilings.
- **Transition:** the head tilts back up to the horizon and we walk toward the end of the pier.
- **Prompt:** `First-person POV, handheld, realistic, one continuous shot, no cuts. Standing at the end of a weathered wooden pier at golden-hour sunset, a small figure in a yellow raincoat with a tall red-tipped antenna cap and red scarf stands at the railing pointing at the sea. Glittering water droplets float upward off the ocean surface. The sea level begins to drop fast, exposing barnacled pilings. Slow forward drift, slight breathing sway, a quick glance down at the retreating waterline. Warm orange light, teal water, stylized low-poly toon look.` — **last frame:** wooden pier edge, ladder visible ahead, sea far below.

### 2. 0:08–0:20 — Ladder down
- **POV:** walk to the pier end, turn, climb down a long hanging ladder, hands (mittens) alternating on the rungs; the water line falls away beneath us and the seabed appears.
- **Transition:** boots touch the sand — the camera levels out into a walk.
- **Prompt:** `First-person POV, handheld, one continuous shot, no cuts. Climbing down a long wooden ladder hand over hand with thick mittens, rungs sliding past the camera, the ocean draining away below to reveal a dark seabed, dusk sky turning violet. Starting from the previous last frame.`

### 3. 0:20–0:32 — Dry seabed walk
- **POV:** first steps on the seabed, Marlo walking ahead with a lantern. Shipwreck on the left, bleaching coral, fish flopping on the sand, time-lapse: sun sets, stars come out.
- **Transition:** the walking stops; breathing slows.
- **Prompt:** `First-person POV walking across a drained ocean floor at dusk, handheld, one continuous shot. A small figure with a lantern walks ahead. A tilted shipwreck on the left, pale coral cones, fish flopping on wet sand, sky time-lapsing from sunset to a starry night. Stylized low-poly toon look.`

### 4. 0:32–0:44 — Look up
- **POV:** we stop; the camera tilts back slowly to a giant Moon; three glittering rivers of water flow up into it. A mitten rises to shield the eyes.
- **Transition:** the head snaps back down, Marlo's lantern swings, a rumble starts.
- **Prompt:** `First-person POV, one continuous shot. Standing still on a dry seabed, slowly tilting the head back to reveal an enormous pale moon with three glittering rivers of water streaming upward into it across a starry violet sky. A gloved hand rises to shield the eyes.`

### 5. 0:44–0:58 — Run back
- **POV:** turn 180°, sprint toward the distant pier that now towers like a lighthouse; the sun spins through days, the sky turns crimson, shake and chromatic aberration build, FOV widens.
- **Transition:** we reach the bottom of the ladder, hand reaches for a rung.
- **Prompt:** `First-person POV sprinting across a dry ocean floor toward a tall wooden pier, heavy handheld shake, sky time-lapsing through days and turning crimson, ground trembling, stars streaking, panic breathing.`

### 6. 0:58–1:05 — Snap & surge
- **POV:** rivers fall from the sky (white flash, dolly-zoom), the ocean slams back, the camera tumbles underwater up along the ladder and **bursts through the surface** (second white flash).
- **Transition:** surface break → gasp → camera settles on the pier.
- **Prompt:** `First-person POV, one continuous shot. Looking up the length of a pier ladder as glittering water rivers collapse from the sky in slow motion, white flash, then a massive wave rises and carries the camera upward underwater with bubbles and tumbling roll, breaking through the surface into sunset light.`

### 7. 1:05–1:12 — Loop
- **POV:** calm sea, Marlo again points at the horizon, droplets begin to rise again — identical to frame 0.
- **Prompt:** `First-person POV on a wooden pier at golden-hour sunset, calm teal ocean, a small figure in a yellow raincoat with a red-tipped antenna points at the sea, droplets lifting from the water. Identical to the opening frame.`
